# Impeccable — PostPlan design context

Persistent notes for AI and humans. Update this when product positioning or visual rules change.

## Design Context

### Users

**Inferred from product copy and features:** People who operate **scheduled outbound HTTP** (JSON webhooks): builders using Make.com, Zapier, or custom endpoints; content operators who **import** from WordPress, RSS, or CSV and need a **calendar** view of what fires when. They are often **repeat visitors** to a dense app (posts, schedules, calendar, reports, settings) rather than casual browsers.

**Job to be done:** Plan payloads, attach schedules, trust that sends happen on time, and **debug** when something fails (reports, statuses).

**Open to confirm:** Primary segment (solo indie vs. small team vs. agency). Whether “confidence under load” or “speed for power users” is the higher priority emotionally.

### Brand Personality

**Inferred from UI:** **Calm, capable, and understated** — a **tooling** aesthetic (not social, not playful SaaS illustration-heavy). Copy on `/welcome` is direct and capability-led (“Schedule posts. Send to webhooks.”). The visual system favors **restraint**: flat surfaces, no drop shadows, teal-on-neutral hierarchy.

**Voice:** Short, instructional headings; muted secondary text for supporting detail. Errors and alerts are **high-contrast and explicit** (WCAG-minded palette), not cute.

**Open to confirm:** Three words you *want* on the tin (e.g. “reliable, minimal, pro”) and any **anti-references** (“don’t look like Notion / Linear / …”).

### Aesthetic Direction

**Theme:** **Light and dark** are both first-class (`:root` / `.dark` tokens in `src/app.css`). App shell: **dark sidebar** (`--sidebar-bg` near-black) with **light content card** on a dark main background on desktop; auth and blocked flows use a **narrow card** on neutral background.

**Palette:** Warm neutrals (`#f4f4f0` family) with **teal primary** (`#005f78`, hover `#007394`). Danger states align with **red** semantic tokens, not primary.

**Typography:** System UI stack in the app (`ui-sans-serif, system-ui, …`). Marketing `/welcome` uses **larger, tighter display** headings; in-app pages use **`.page-title` / `.page-lead`** or `PageSectionHeading` — don’t casually mix ad-hoc `text-2xl` page chrome without reason.

**Motion:** Snappy, short durations (`--motion-instant`, `--motion-snappy`, `--motion-panel`) with **`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`** collapsing animations/transitions globally.

**Open to confirm:** Any **mandatory** brand colors or fonts beyond what’s in `app.css`; whether marketing pages should stay visually bolder than the app or gradually converge.

### Design Principles

1. **Semantic tokens first** — Use `--text`, `--surface`, `--primary`, `--border`, sidebar tokens, and shared classes (`.content-card`, `.btn-primary`, `.btn-danger-outline`, `.btn-touch`, `.page-title`, `.page-lead`) before inventing new hex/rgb or one-off button styles.
2. **Flat, intentional surfaces** — Project explicitly neutralizes Tailwind shadows; depth comes from **border, contrast, and spacing**, not elevation shadows.
3. **Accessible controls** — Visible **`:focus-visible`** rings on interactive elements; **44px minimum** touch targets for primary actions (`.btn-touch`); alerts/badges documented as **WCAG AA–minded** in CSS comments — preserve contrast when changing colors.
4. **Density with clarity** — The app is **data-heavy** (tables, calendar, logs); favor **scannable hierarchy**, **truncation/wrap helpers** (e.g. `.overflow-wrap-anywhere`) for user-generated content, and **consistent page headers** (`PageSectionHeading`) so every route feels familiar.
5. **Respect route context** — Full marketing layout on `/welcome`; **auth/blocked** centered narrow column; main app **sidebar + content card** — don’t force one layout pattern everywhere.

### Implementation anchors

- Global styles and tokens: `src/app.css`
- App shell and layout rules: `src/routes/+layout.svelte`
- Shared heading primitive: `src/lib/components/PageSectionHeading.svelte`
- Empty states: `src/lib/components/EmptyState.svelte`
- Theme init: `$lib/stores/theme.js`
